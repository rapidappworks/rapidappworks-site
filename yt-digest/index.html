Great ideas.
Limited time.

YouTube is full of useful lectures, tutorials, and conversations. But the information you need can be buried inside a long video, and finding it takes time.

YT Digest helps you work with the video’s transcript so you can understand the ideas and choose which moments deserve a closer look.

One video. A different way in.

Start with
the transcript.

YT Digest opens a YouTube video’s available transcript beside the player. You can read the words at your own pace, search for a topic, and copy or download the text.

Choose a clean transcript without timestamps for reading, or keep timestamps so you can find your way back to the original explanation.

Works with videos that have available subtitles.

Bring it into
your ChatGPT.

Send the transcript directly into a new conversation in your own ChatGPT account. YT Digest prepares the text in the message composer, with room for your custom prompt on the last line.

Ask for a short explanation, a summary, or help exploring a specific topic. Customize your prompt, then press Send when you’re ready.

Your ChatGPT.
Your possibilities.

YT Digest works with the ChatGPT account you already use. It does not require an API key, a separate AI subscription, or pay-per-token API credits.

Your available ChatGPT context and tools can help you take the transcript further.

Memory

Pick up
where you left off.

Use the transcript along with your memories. ChatGPT can be guided to use your memories for analyzing the transcripts.

Projects

Bring it into
your project.

Invoke ChatGPT to use the transcript inside your project context to achieve your objectives.

Connected apps

Use your
connected apps.

ChatGPT can be made to edit your docs and sheets present inside your Google Drive based on transcript details. Similarly, it can work with any connected apps.

Plugins

Use plugins.

ChatGPT plugins, similar to connected apps, can be used to analyze the transcript or generate content from it. For example, a PowerPoint plugin can generate a slide based on the transcript.

Skills

Use the process
you already trust.

Use an available skill to apply your preferred process to the transcript. For example, ask ChatGPT to turn the ideas into class slides using your presentation skill.

Ask where it happened.
Jump straight to it.

Chrome Split View keeps YouTube and ChatGPT side by side. Send a timestamped transcript and ask where the speaker discussed the topic you want to explore.

When ChatGPT includes timestamp references in its response, YT Digest turns those times into green, dotted-underlined links. Click a time to jump the paired video to that moment and hear the explanation in context.

Try the timestamp link in the conversation.
